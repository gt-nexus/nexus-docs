# Quick Start Guide

## New to ACCESS? Start here

Welcome! Before you can log in to Nexus for the first time, you need an ACCESS ID and an allocation on Nexus:

1. [Create an ACCESS ID](https://operations.access-ci.org/identity/new-user)
2. [Request an ACCESS allocation](https://allocations.access-ci.org/get-your-first-project) on Nexus
3. Watch for an email with your Nexus username
4. [Log in to Nexus](nexus-docs/access/login.md)

## Already have an ACCESS ID? Start here

If you've requested allocations on other ACCESS resources before, getting started on Nexus works the same way:

1. Sign in with your ACCESS ID and [exchange your credits an allocation](https://allocations.access-ci.org/get-your-first-project) on Nexus
2. Watch for an email with your Nexus username
3. [Log in to Nexus](nexus-docs/access/login.md)
